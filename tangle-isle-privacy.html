Tangle Isle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Tangle Isle is a puzzle game for Android published by Samal Studio, the developer name of Shubhransu Kumar Samal, an individual developer in India. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information ourselves. The game has no account or sign-in and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Information kept on your phone

Your progress (level, coins, island, pets, settings and stats) is saved only on your own phone. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows optional reward videos: you choose to watch a short video ad to get an extra heart, a free hint, double coins or a bigger daily gift. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location based on your IP address
  • device and app information, such as phone model, operating system, language and app version
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps, and crash and performance data

This information is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Ads are always optional. You never have to watch one to play.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices for ads.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings: Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads on some phones).

Internet use

The puzzles work offline. The game uses the internet only to load reward videos and to show the consent message where required.

Sharing

If you tap a share button, the game opens your phone's own share menu with a picture or text you choose to send. We never see what you share or who you share it with.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load reward videos.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Vibration: short feedback when a move is blocked. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

Tangle Isle is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and want information removed, contact us and we will help, including by contacting Google where needed.

Deleting your data

Game progress is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Advertising data is held by Google; you can reset your advertising ID as described above or contact us for help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and the date above will change.
